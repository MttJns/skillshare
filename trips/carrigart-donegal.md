# Carrigart & Rosguill Peninsula, County Donegal

> A compact Gaeltacht village on Sheephaven Bay — your base for the Wild Atlantic Way's most dramatic northern stretches.

---

## Getting There

**By car from Dublin Airport:** ~3h 50min via M1 → N2/N14 → Letterkenny → R245 north.  
A car is essential — local public transport is minimal.

**By bus:** [Bus Éireann Expressway Route 30](https://www.buseireann.ie/routes-and-timetables/30) Dublin → Donegal Town (~3h 45min), then [McGinley Coaches](https://www.mcginleycoaches.com) onward to Creeslough (8km from Carrigart).

## Beaches

### Carrigart Beach
Soft golden sand along Sheephaven Bay. Calm and scenic for walks and picnics — strong tidal currents make swimming unsafe here.  
![Carrigart Beach](https://www.donegaldayout.ie/cdn/shop/articles/Carrigart_Beach.jpg)  
[More info](https://www.donegaldayout.ie/pages/carrigart-beach)

### Downings Beach
3km from Carrigart. Sandy, sheltered, and safe for swimming with views across to the Ards Peninsula.

### Murder Hole Beach *(Boyeeghter Bay)*
One of Donegal's most dramatic and photogenic hidden beaches — a steep path leads down to a secluded cove. Not suitable for swimming. Worth the hike purely for the scenery.  
[![Murder Hole Beach](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/39/Murder_Hole_Beach_Donegal.jpg/1280px-Murder_Hole_Beach_Donegal.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyeeghter_Bay)

### Marble Hill Strand
Golden sand, clear Atlantic water, popular with families.  
[Marble Hill Beach info](https://www.donegalcoco.ie/services/environment/beaches/marbelhillbeach/)

### Tramore Beach (Rosguill)
On the Atlantic Drive loop, facing open ocean — wild and exposed, great for surfers and photographers.

## Drives & Walks

### Atlantic Drive — Rosguill Peninsula *(12km loop)*
Arguably the most scenic short drive in Donegal. Hugs the clifftops of the Rosguill Peninsula with views of Sheephaven Bay, Mulroy Bay, Trá na Rossan beach, and the open Atlantic.  
[Wild Atlantic Way — Rosguill](https://www.wildatlanticway.com/discover/counties/donegal)

### Ards Forest Park
Woodland trails, sand dunes, saltwater lakes, and golden beach on the Ards Peninsula. The **Binngorm Trail** (2h loop) gives sweeping views over Sheephaven Bay.  
[Coillte — Ards Forest Park](https://www.coillte.ie/site/ards-forest-park/)

### Glen Walk
One of the longer scenic trails in the area, running through glacial Glen valley.  
[AllTrails — Carrigart area](https://www.alltrails.com/ireland/county-donegal/carrigart)

## History & Culture

### Doe Castle
16th-century tower house fortress built by the MacSweeney clan on a promontory jutting into Sheephaven Bay — surrounded by the sea on three sides and a rock-cut moat on the fourth. Guided tours in summer.  
[![Doe Castle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/58/Doe_Castle_-_geograph.org.uk_-_1373060.jpg/1280px-Doe_Castle_-_geograph.org.uk_-_1373060.jpg)](https://heritageireland.ie/visit/places-to-visit/doe-castle/)  
[Heritage Ireland — Doe Castle](https://heritageireland.ie/visit/places-to-visit/doe-castle/)

### Fanad Lighthouse
Ireland's second-most-northerly lighthouse, now a visitor centre with accommodation available. Panoramic views across Lough Swilly and Mulroy Bay.  
[Fanad Lighthouse](https://www.fanadlighthouse.com)

### Glenveagh National Park & Castle
~45min drive south. A Victorian castle set in 16,500 hectares of wild Derryveagh Mountains with a formal walled garden. Guided tours only inside the castle.  
[Glenveagh National Park](https://www.glenveaghnationalpark.ie)

### Island Roy
3 miles from Carrigart, connected by causeway at low tide. A Wild Atlantic Way Discovery Point — notable for red-tinged vegetation in winter and nesting seabirds.

### Carrigart as Gaeltacht
The village is in an Irish-speaking district. Signs, place names, and some locals converse in Irish — a living piece of Gaelic culture.

## Best Time to Visit

| Season | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **June–August** | Long evenings, warmest weather, festivals | Busiest, book ahead |
| **May / September** | Quieter, good weather, lower prices | Some businesses shorter hours |
| **Winter** | Dramatic stormy seas, almost no crowds | Many places closed, cold and wet |

**Carrigart Summer Festival:** usually runs mid-August — music, crafts, and food on the village green.
